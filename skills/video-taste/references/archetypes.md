# Archetype recipes

Beat counts are starting points. Pick the BPM first, then the counts; a scene's length in seconds is `beats × 60 / bpm`.

## Demo launch (`archetype: demo`) · 16:9 · 96 BPM · 45–75s

| # | Scene | Beats | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `logo` | 3 | Mark alone on white. Breathe. |
| 2 | `typeTitle` "Introducing *Name*" | 5 | Accent the name. |
| 3 | `typeTitle` the one-line promise | 7–8 | ≤ 7 words. |
| 4 | `promptBox` or `uiShot` the first action | 8 | Start zoomed on the input (1.6–1.8×), pull back as it types, click send. |
| 5 | `bracketTitle` "Go from [x] to (y)" | 6 | The transformation in two nouns. |
| 6 | `uiShot` the result | 8–10 | `space` backdrop; push in on the proof, then on the outcome. |
| 7 | `typeTitle` second claim | 6–8 | |
| 8 | `uiShot` second proof | 8–10 | Different backdrop from #6. |
| 9 | `cardGrid` breadth | 6–8 | Many outputs drifting; energy peaks here. |
| 10 | `bracketTitle` closing line | 6–7 | |
| 11 | `logoEnd` | 6–8 | Tagline + availability footnote. |

## Feature announcement (`archetype: feature`) · 16:9 or 1:1 · 100 BPM · 20–45s

`typeTitle` (the feature name, accented) → `promptBox` or `uiShot` (proof 1) → `bracketTitle` (the claim) → `uiShot` (proof 2, tighter zoom) → `logoEnd` with "Available today in …" footnote. Reuse the product's own UI chrome; no space backdrops unless the brand already uses them.

## Brand teaser (`archetype: teaser`) · 1:1 or 16:9 · 120–136 BPM · 15–25s

| # | Scene | Beats | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `montage` one calm item (`pacing: steady`, `from: 4`) | 4 | The bookend frame. Energy ~0.15. |
| 2 | `montage` with the motif (`mask: glyph` / `lens` / `horizon`) | 16 | `pacing: accelerate`, `from: 1`, `to: 0.25`. 12–20 distinct dense materials or images. |
| 3 | `montage` with `overlay` words | 8 | 2–3 word groups, ½ → ¼ beat shots. |
| 4 | `titleCard` | 4–5 | Hit lands here. Color from the montage. |
| 5 | `logoEnd` | 6–8 | Back to the opening frame (bookend). |

Glyph masks need dense materials (dots, mosaic, halftone, stripes, rings, gradient, cells, waves, photos). Sparse ones (constellation, blueprint, topo, type) lose the silhouette.

## Paid social ad (`archetype: ad`) · 9:16 or 1:1 · 110–125 BPM · 6–15s

The claim must land in the first 1.5s (no slow open). `typeTitle` claim (3 beats) → `uiShot` or `montage` proof (8 beats) → `titleCard` with the offer (4 beats) → `logoEnd` with the CTA as `tagline` (4 beats). Keep text in the center 80% for platform UI overlays.

## Changelog clip · 16:9 · 100 BPM · 10–20s

`typeTitle` "New in *Product*" → one `uiShot` with two camera moves and a cursor click → `logoEnd`. Skip the logo opener.
