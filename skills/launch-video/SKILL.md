# Make a launch video

You are the director. The output is a finished, critic-passed MP4 plus the spec that made it. Work in this order and do not skip the looking.

Paths below are relative to the Slate repo root. First-time setup: `cd studio && npm install`, and `python3 -m pip install numpy scipy` (plus `librosa` for reference study and beat grids). Rendering needs ffmpeg and a Chromium (Remotion downloads one, or set `SLATE_CHROME`).

## 1. Brief

Get, or decide and state: product, audience, the one thing that's new, length, aspect ratio(s), where it will be posted, brand kit (fonts, colors, logo), and any must-say lines. If the person gave a product URL or repo, read it: the tone of the product decides the film.

## 2. Treatment (load `video-taste` first)

Write five lines before any JSON:

1. **Family and archetype** (demo, feature, teaser, ad, changelog) and why.
2. **The one idea** in five words.
3. **The motif** that repeats (a UI control, a glyph, a lens, a horizon, a color).
4. **Tempo and music style** (`glass` for calm demos, `pulse` for teasers and ads) and the energy curve.
5. **The ending** (bookend frame, tagline, footnote).

Then a beat sheet: one row per scene with type, beats, on-screen text and what the viewer should feel. Use the recipes in `skills/video-taste/references/archetypes.md`.

## 3. Assets

- **Product UI:** capture real screens at 2× with `node studio/scripts/capture.mjs <url-or-html> examples/<name>/assets/<shot>.png --width 1440 --height 900`. Prefer real product URLs. When a screen does not exist yet, build a faithful HTML mock in `examples/<name>/ui/` with the product's real fonts and colors (see `examples/slate-launch/ui/`).
- **Footage and imagery:** put images in `examples/<name>/assets/` and reference them as `{"image": "assets/x.jpg"}` in montage or card items. Only use material you have the rights to (public-domain archives, the brand's own photography, generated images). Otherwise use built-in materials.
- **Music:** generated by default. For a supplied track, run `python3 tools/beatgrid.py track.mp3` and copy its `bpm` and `offset` into the spec.

## 4. Spec

Create `examples/<name>/spec.json` (full reference: `docs/spec.md`). Every scene length in `beats`. Start from the closest example (`examples/slate-launch` for demos, `examples/slate-teaser` for teasers) rather than from scratch. Camera keyframes and cursor points are in image-normalized coordinates: (0.5, 0.5) is the center, zoom 1 frames the whole window.

## 5. Look before you render

```bash
cd studio
node scripts/render.mjs ../examples/<name> --stills 1,4,8,12 --scale 0.5
```

Open the PNGs in `examples/<name>/build/stills/`. Check focal point, type size, crop of every camera keyframe. Fix the spec, repeat. Stills are cheap; renders are not.

## 6. Render and critique (load `video-critic`)

```bash
node scripts/render.mjs ../examples/<name>
```

This writes the timeline, the score (`build/mix.wav`, mastered), the MP4 in `out/`, and the critic report in `build/critic/`. Then look at `build/critic/contact.jpg` yourself and score it with the rubric in `video-critic`. Fix and re-render until the critic passes and every rubric line is at least a 4. Usually 2–3 rounds; stop after 4 and say what is still weak.

## 7. Deliver

Hand over the MP4, a poster frame (`ffmpeg -ss <t> -i out/<name>.mp4 -frames:v 1 poster.png`), the critic report, and two sentences: the idea, and what you'd change with more time or real footage. For extra aspect ratios, copy the spec, change `meta.width/height`, re-check stills (type size and camera framing change), and render again.

## Rules

- Never invent product capabilities, metrics or quotes for a real product. Mock data in UI shots must be clearly sample data or approved by the person.
- Never use another company's logo, footage or music without permission.
- Keep the person's brand. The taste guide is about restraint and structure, not a house style.
